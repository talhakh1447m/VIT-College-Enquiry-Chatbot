Scholarship Schemes & Eligibility Framework

MahaDBT Social Welfare Concessions for Vidyalankar Students
EBC / SEBC / EWS

Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna

Provides 50% concession on tuition fees for students admitted through CAP general merit whose total annual family income from all sources does not exceed ₹8,00,000.

Key Documents Required: Income Certificate from Tahsildar (≤ ₹8 Lakhs), Domicile Certificate of Maharashtra, CAP Allotment Letter.
SC / ST / VJNT / SBC / OBC

Government of India Post-Matric Scholarship & Free-ship

100% tuition fee reimbursement for SC/ST students, and category-based tuition subsidies for VJNT/SBC/OBC students as per Maharashtra Social Justice Department norms.

Key Documents Required: Caste Certificate, Caste Validity Certificate, Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate valid for the current academic session.
TFWS Seats

AICTE Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS)

Supernumerary seats created up to 5% of sanctioned intake. Students pay zero tuition fee throughout all 4 years. Allotted strictly on MHT-CET general merit in CAP rounds.

Eligibility: Opted for TFWS choice code during CAP option entry; annual family income ≤ ₹8 Lakhs.
